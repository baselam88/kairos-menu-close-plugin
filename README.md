# Menu Close Builder

Builds the two-configuration close: unsell what they don't need (out loud, with the reason), prescribe what their constraint requires, then an A/B choice with one recommendation.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Both A and B must be configurations happily delivered — a decoy B designed to lose is forbidden. A third option turns it into homework, not a close.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Menu Close Builder to help me: builds the two-configuration close: unsell what they don't need (out loud, with the reason), prescribe what their constraint requires, then an A/B choice with one recommendation — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
